📊 Productivity Score

🔢
How it's calculated
Each day gets a raw score out of 100 based on three pillars:
• Habits — % of your habits completed × 40 points
• Tasks — tasks done (capped at 5) × 6 points each = max 30
• Focus — minutes focused (capped at 120 min) ÷ 4 = max 30

Your displayed score is a 14-day exponentially weighted rolling average. Recent days carry ~4× more weight than days 7 days ago, so your score responds quickly to improvement and drops within days of slacking — making 100 genuinely achievable with ~7 days of strong effort.
🏷
Score labels
0–20: Just Starting  ·  21–40: Building Habits  ·  41–60: Gaining Ground  ·  61–75: Strong Momentum  ·  76–90: High Achiever  ·  91–100: Peak State 🔥

🔥 Streaks

🔥
Habit Streak
Consecutive days where every single habit you've added was completed. Missing even one habit resets it to zero. This is the hardest streak to maintain — and the most powerful.
—
✓
Task Streak
Consecutive days where you completed at least one task. Encourages showing up every day, even with small wins.
—
📓
Journal Streak
Consecutive days with at least one journal entry. Writing daily builds self-awareness and sharpens thinking.
—
⏱
Focus Streak
Consecutive days with at least one completed focus (Pomodoro) session. Deep work every day compounds dramatically over months.

🌱 Habits

📅
Monthly Grid
Each column is a day of the month. A filled cell (✓) means you completed that habit. Navigate backwards to see your history across months.
🎯
Goal vs Achieved
Set a monthly goal for each habit (default 20 days). The Achieved column turns:
• Green — you met your goal
• Yellow — you're at 65–99% of goal
• Red — below 65%
📈
Per-habit individual streak
The 🔥 badge on each habit row shows that habit's current consecutive-day streak, tracked independently from all others.

✓ Tasks

🗂
Three sections
Active — tasks not yet done and not past their due date.
Overdue — tasks with a due date that has already passed.
Completed — tasks you've checked off.
🏷
Groups
Create named groups (e.g. Work, Personal, Health) to organise tasks. Filter by group using the tabs above the task list.
📅
Due dates & times
Assign a specific date and time to any task. Tasks become Overdue automatically when that moment passes.

⏱ Focus

🍅
Pomodoro cycle
25 min focus → 5 min break, repeat 4 times → 15 min long break. The dots below the timer show your position in the current cycle.
📊
Focus score contribution
Every minute of focus time contributes to your daily productivity score. 120 minutes = full 30 points. Sessions are logged and visible on the Focus page.

📓 Journal

✍️
Dated entries
Each entry is tied to a specific date. You can backdate entries by changing the date field — useful for capturing yesterday's thoughts today.
🔥
Journal streak
Writing daily, even briefly, builds a reflection habit. Multiple entries on the same day still count as one streak day.
